SPECK
You are the smallest mage in your class. At the bottom of the rankings list. You know, the kind of person everyone knows would not stand a chance against another in a proper showdown.

But you discovered a secret room under the whole city.

Somewhere behind that door is something that belongs to you, with five enemies of formidable strength standing in your way. And there you are, climbing down the stairs despite being the last person to survive so far.

SPECK is a battle game inspired by the Jujutsu Kaisen anime where you will have to battle stronger and stronger opponents while mastering cursed techniques and winning unfair fights.

Select one of many fighters, fight, apply some cursed techniques and expand your Domain when things get really rough. But brute force is not the only weapon here. You must master the timing and unleash Black Flashes. Before a battle, make a vow in order to get extra power, but do not forget about consequences.
